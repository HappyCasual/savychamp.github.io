The Solo Developer Journey

Building mobile games as a solo developer is both challenging and rewarding. From our experience, one person handles everything — coding, design, testing, and updates.

Multiple Roles

A solo developer works as programmer, designer, tester, and marketer. This requires strong focus and discipline.

Learning Process

We learned game development step by step through experimentation and testing.

Challenges

Handling everything alone is difficult, but it improves skills quickly.

Our Experience

We noticed that simple games perform better because they are easier to build and test.

Conclusion

Consistency and learning are key to success in solo development.
