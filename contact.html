Contact SavyChamp

Hello there! Welcome to the SavyChamp contact page. First of all, I want to say a big thank you for playing our games and visiting our website. SavyChamp is a small independent mobile app and game development studio running from Andhra Pradesh, India. Because I am a solo developer, the connection I have with my players and website visitors is very important to me. I always love to hear your thoughts, honest feedback, and fresh ideas.

Why You Should Reach Out to Us

Whether you have a simple doubt, a suggestion to make our games better, or you just want to say hi, please feel free to drop a message. Making simple, fun, and relaxing games is my main goal, and your valuable feedback helps me do exactly that. If you loved playing games like Love Merge, Emoji Sentence, or Jig Cards, do let me know! It gives me a lot of motivation to create more free and lightweight apps for you all to enjoy.

Bug Reports and Technical Support

Sometimes, even after testing everything carefully, a small bug, glitch, or error might come up in the app. If you face any problem while playing our games, please do not worry at all. Just send me an email. To help me solve your problem faster, kindly mention a few details in your message. Tell me the name of the game, the mobile phone model you are using, and a little explanation of the issue you are facing. If possible, you can also attach a screenshot. This will help me fix the issue in the next update so that you can experience a smooth and lag-free gaming journey again.

New Ideas and Feature Requests

Do you have a great idea for a new puzzle game? Or maybe you want a new useful feature in the Tamcall app? I am always open to new ideas. As an independent developer, I listen closely to what my users want. Many of the improvements in our apps come directly from the excellent suggestions given by users like you. So, never hesitate to share what is on your mind.

Business and Collaborations

If you are a fellow developer, a publisher, or someone looking for a business collaboration, you are most welcome to reach out. I am open to discussing cross-promotions, advertising partnerships, or simply sharing knowledge about mobile app development and design.

How to Contact Me

You can reach out to me anytime through our official email address. Since I manage design, coding, and support all by myself, keeping all messages in one single inbox helps me stay organized. Whether it is a bug report, a business query, or general feedback, please send it to the email below.

Email: contact@savychamp.com

Response Time

Since SavyChamp is handled entirely by a single person, please allow me 24 to 48 hours to read and reply to your emails. I check my inbox daily and I promise to get back to you as soon as possible. My working hours follow Indian Standard Time (IST).

Thank you once again for your love and support. Keep playing, stay relaxed, and have a wonderful day ahead!
