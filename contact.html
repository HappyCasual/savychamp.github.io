Email Support

If you are facing any issue while using our website or playing our games, you can contact our support team directly. We recommend including complete details such as your device type, browser, game name, and a clear explanation of the issue. This helps us identify and resolve your problem faster.

Response Time

We usually respond to emails within 24 to 48 hours. However, response time may vary slightly depending on the volume of requests. During weekends or holidays, replies may take a little longer, but rest assured that we aim to respond to every genuine query.

To help us reply faster, please keep your message clear and structured. Avoid sending multiple emails for the same issue, as it may delay the process. We appreciate your patience and understanding.

About SavyChamp

SavyChamp is an independent digital platform focused on creating simple, relaxing, and engaging mobile gaming experiences. Our goal is to build games that are easy to understand, enjoyable to play, and accessible to users of all age groups.

We focus on casual and stress-free gameplay that helps users relax and refresh their minds. Every game is designed with user experience in mind, ensuring smooth performance, clean design, and intuitive controls. We continuously test and improve our products based on real user feedback.

Our platform is built with a long-term vision of providing quality entertainment without unnecessary complexity. We believe that simple ideas can create the most enjoyable experiences, and that is the philosophy behind everything we create.
