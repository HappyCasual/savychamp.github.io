Who is behind SavyChamp?

Hi there! I am a solo independent game developer based in India. I started SavyChamp because I wanted to create mobile games that are genuinely fun, relaxing, and easy for anyone to pick up and play.

I know how frustrating it is when a game lags or drains your battery. That is why I work hard to make sure every SavyChamp game runs perfectly smooth, even on older or low-end smartphones. Everyone deserves a great gaming experience without buying an expensive phone!
