Gameplay Overview

The core gameplay of Jig Cards revolves around rearranging shuffled cards to recreate a complete image. Each puzzle is presented as a grid of visual cards, and your task is to swap them into their correct positions.

Unlike traditional jigsaw puzzles where pieces are placed individually, this game simplifies the interaction by allowing players to swap entire cards. This creates a faster and smoother experience while still maintaining the challenge of pattern recognition and logical thinking.

Each level introduces a new image, offering variety and keeping the experience fresh. As you progress, the puzzles become more complex, requiring better observation and planning.

How to Play

  • View the scrambled grid of image cards
  • Tap and swap cards to reposition them
  • Match cards correctly to form the complete image
  • Use hints if you get stuck
  • Complete the puzzle to unlock new artwork
