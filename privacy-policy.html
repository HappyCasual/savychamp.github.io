Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 20, 2026

Welcome to SavyChamp. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile apps, games, website, and services.

SavyChamp is an independent mobile game and app studio based in India. We create simple, relaxing, and lightweight apps for users around the world. Some of our apps include Jig Cards: Multi Art Puzzle, Love Merge, Emoji Sentence Puzzle, Tamcall, Arrows Paint ASMR, Panda Sort, and other future apps and games released by us.

We always try to keep our apps safe, simple, and user friendly. We also respect your personal privacy. We do not sell your personal information to others. We only collect limited information that helps us improve app performance, fix problems, and provide a better user experience.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our apps or website, some basic information may be collected automatically. Most of this information is non-personal and is used only for app improvement and analytics.

The information we may collect includes:

  • Device model and phone type
  • Android version or operating system version
  • Country and language settings
  • App version
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Gameplay progress and app usage details
  • Advertising ID for ads and analytics
  • Basic performance data like loading speed and memory usage

This information helps us understand how users interact with our apps. For example, if many users face lag on a certain phone model, we can improve optimization in future updates.

We do not collect sensitive personal details like passwords, bank information, or private photos through our apps.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information mainly to improve app quality and provide a better experience for users.

Information may be used for:

  • Improving app performance
  • Fixing bugs and crashes
  • Optimizing games for low-end devices
  • Understanding which features users enjoy most
  • Showing advertisements in free apps
  • Preventing fraud or misuse
  • Improving security and stability

We always try to keep our apps lightweight, smooth, and battery friendly. Usage data helps us continue improving the overall experience.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Some SavyChamp apps may show advertisements because ads help support free app development.

We currently use third-party advertising and analytics services such as:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

These services may collect limited device information or advertising identifiers to show relevant ads and improve app services.

Third-party services may use cookies, advertising IDs, or similar technologies to personalize ads based on your interests and app activity.

You can learn more about how Google handles user data here:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Please note that third-party services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reading their policies if you want more details about how they handle data.

4. Google AdSense and Website Ads

Our website may also use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to websites or apps.

Personalized ads help show more relevant advertisements to users. However, you can manage ad preferences directly through your Google account settings.

We do not personally control the exact ads shown on our website or apps.

5. Children's Privacy

Our apps are made for a general audience and are designed to be family friendly whenever possible.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will take steps to delete that information as quickly as possible.

Parents or guardians who believe their child has shared personal information can contact us anytime.

6. Data Security

We care about user safety and take reasonable steps to protect collected information.

While no internet service can guarantee 100% security, we try our best to use trusted services and safe development practices to reduce risks.

However, users should understand that no method of electronic storage or internet transfer is completely secure.

7. External Links

Our apps or website may contain links to third-party websites like Google Play, YouTube, or external services.

Once you leave our website or app and visit another service, their own privacy policy and terms will apply.

We are not responsible for third-party websites, apps, or services.

8. User Choices and Controls

Users can control certain permissions and settings directly from their device settings.

For example, you can:

  • Reset your advertising ID
  • Turn off personalized ads
  • Manage app permissions
  • Clear app data or uninstall apps anytime

Most of our apps work without needing unnecessary permissions.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if our apps, services, or legal requirements change.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

We recommend checking this page occasionally to stay updated.
