Tamcall - Smart Tambola Caller

Tamcall is a simple and smart Tambola (Housie) caller app designed to make your game nights easier, smoother, and more fun.

Digital Solution for Tambola

Forget bulky boards and missing number coins. Tamcall replaces everything with a simple mobile experience.

Easy to Use

Just tap the screen to generate a random number. The app handles everything automatically.

Clear Number Display

Large and clear numbers make it easy for everyone to see, including elderly players.

History Tracking

You can easily check previously called numbers to avoid confusion and verify winners.

Fair Gameplay

The app uses a random number system to ensure fair and unbiased results.

Lightweight and Smooth

Tamcall works smoothly on all devices including low-end smartphones and does not consume much battery.

Why Users Love Tamcall

Users love Tamcall because it makes Tambola simple, removes manual effort, and provides a clean and stress-free experience.

Perfect for All Occasions

You can use Tamcall for family gatherings, parties, festivals, and casual get-togethers.

Conclusion

Tamcall is the easiest way to host a Tambola game with zero hassle and maximum enjoyment.
