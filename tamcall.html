Tamcall - Your Smart Tambola & Housie Caller

Hello friends! While SavyChamp is mostly known for creating relaxing brain puzzle games, we also love making useful apps that bring families and friends together. In India, no festival, family function, or weekend get-together is truly complete without a fun game of Tambola (also known as Housie or Bingo in some places). To make your game nights even better, we created Tamcall—a smart, simple, and digital number caller app for your mobile phone.

The Headache of the Old Physical Board

We all know the struggle of playing with a traditional physical Tambola board. First of all, the box is bulky and hard to carry around. Secondly, the small plastic coins or wooden numbers always seem to go missing. You open the box, and suddenly number 42 and number 89 are lost forever! Sometimes, a kid might accidentally bump into the board, and all the placed numbers get completely mixed up.

Also, the person who is assigned to call out the numbers (the "caller") often gets tired of shouting the numbers loudly so that everyone in the room can hear. Tracking the drawn numbers on a plastic board can lead to confusion and arguments. We realized that in today's digital age, playing Housie should be completely stress-free. That is exactly why Tamcall was born.

What Exactly is Tamcall?

Tamcall is a digital solution that completely replaces the physical board and the plastic coins. All you need are your paper tickets and a pen to strike off the numbers. The app takes care of the rest! It acts as an automated number caller right from your smartphone. When you tap the screen, the app generates a completely random number from 1 to 90 and displays it beautifully on your phone screen.

Top Features Designed for Everyone

Because Tambola is played by everyone—from small children to grandparents—we designed Tamcall to be extremely easy to use. Here are some of the best features you will enjoy:

  • Big and Clear Display: The numbers are shown in a very large, high-contrast font. Even elderly family members with weak eyesight can easily read the numbers from a distance without straining their eyes.
  • Digital History Board: Did someone just shout "Jaldi Five" or "Full House"? No problem! Tamcall has a built-in history board. You can instantly check all the numbers that have been called out so far. This makes it super easy to verify winning tickets and stops any kind of arguments or cheating during the game.
  • 100% Fair Play: Nobody likes a biased game. Our app uses a "True Random Number Generator" algorithm. This simply means every single number has an equal chance of coming out, just like shaking a bag of coins. There is no cheating involved.
  • No Missing Pieces: Because the board is inside your phone, you will never have to worry about losing a number coin ever again. Your game is always ready in your pocket.

Perfect for Any Occasion

Tamcall is a highly versatile app. You can use it anywhere and everywhere. Are you going on a long train journey with your college friends? Just open the app and start playing. Are you hosting a ladies' kitty party in your apartment? Let Tamcall do the calling while you enjoy your snacks. It is also the perfect companion for grand festival celebrations like Diwali, New Year's Eve, or summer vacation family reunions. You can even connect your phone to a Bluetooth speaker so the whole hall can hear the numbers clearly!

How to Host a Game with Tamcall

Hosting a game is now a piece of cake. Just follow these simple steps:

  1. Distribute Tambola paper tickets and pens to all the players sitting in the room.
  2. Decide the winning prizes (like Top Line, Middle Line, Bottom Line, Four Corners, and the grand Full House).
  3. Open the Tamcall app on your mobile phone.
  4. Tap the screen to draw the first number, and announce it to the group. Keep drawing until someone claims a prize.
  5. Use the history board to quickly cross-check the numbers when someone wins. It is that simple!

Smooth, Lightweight, and Battery Friendly

As an independent developer, I know that not everyone has an expensive, high-end mobile phone. That is why Tamcall is heavily optimized. It is a very lightweight application that takes up very little storage space on your device. It runs flawlessly even on older Android phones with just 3GB or 4GB of RAM. Furthermore, because Tambola games can go on for hours, the app is designed to be battery-friendly. It will not drain your battery quickly, and it will not cause your phone to heat up in your hands.
