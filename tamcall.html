Tamcall (Tambola / Housie Caller)

Introduction

Tamcall is a modern digital Tambola, Housie, and Bingo caller app designed to simplify gameplay with smooth and accurate number generation.

Gameplay Overview

The app generates random numbers with audio and keeps track of history.

How to Use

  • Start the app
  • Tap play
  • Use auto/manual mode

Features

  • ✔ Random number generator
  • ✔ Multi-language audio
  • ✔ Offline support
